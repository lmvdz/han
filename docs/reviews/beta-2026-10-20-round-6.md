## What the implementation fixes and what it does not

PR 183 replaces the unconditional approval claim with neutral acceptance wording. Its local effect query is a bounded, read-only presentation hint, not a new authorization path. Manual-wait hints require an open effect, no recorded approvals and no proposer-sufficient policy. Unknown/stale/missing views remain neutral. Proposal generations and IDs prevent older queued/accepted/refused callbacks from replacing newer proposal state; general blocked workspace notices survive late callbacks until a new request begins. The lane remains the source of progress when it has reported one.

PR 184 adds default-false measurement provenance to the status payload. A price table alone is insufficient: unpriced calls, missing usage, malformed/truncated SSE, cancellation and in-flight calls prevent complete-cost reporting. Ledger amount and completeness are read under one lock; a completed accounting gap remains sticky. ACP metadata is used only for subscription token reporting and cannot overwrite API-key proxy cost. A proposal omits cost when complete accounting is unavailable.

Cost views retain missing agent reports as unknown, preserve each session's original budget unit, keep unlike-unit subtotals separate and mark overflow explicitly. Updated TUI rendering labels incomplete/missing cost as unmeasured. The extension is additive and carries no authority; legacy clients ignoring it can still display a misleading zero. This is host-reported accounting based on configured prices, not a provider invoice or a guarantee that unpriced calls are financially capped.

The malformed-SSE completeness and mixed-unit overflow issues found during review were corrected and retained as regressions before this assessment. These fixes improve truthful reporting; they do not establish new billing-provider integration, remote onboarding or cross-platform harness support.
